SmartExpense

Your private daily expense tracker.
Connect your own Google Sheet — your data stays in your Google account, invisible to everyone else.

1
Open Google Sheets and create a new blank spreadsheet.
2
Click Extensions → Apps Script, paste the Code.gs contents, and save.
3
Click Deploy → New Deployment. Type: Web App. Execute as: Me. Access: Anyone. Copy the Web App URL.
4
Paste your Web App URL below and click Connect.
